1. Purpose of This Portal

The sole objective of StudySignal is to index, organize, and provide direct, verified hyperlinked access to publicly disclosed educational documents (such as academic curricula, syllabus PDF files, official model papers, past question papers, timetables, and administrative circulars) released by India's statutory education boards in the public domain.

3. Handling of Demo, Model Paper & Missing Data

In accordance with our strict data transparency policies:

  • We do not invent or fabricate exam dates, notifications, PYQ links, or circular numbers.
  • Official Model Papers (such as BSEB 2026 Model Question Papers or UPMSP Model Papers) are explicitly separated from Previous Year Question Papers (PYQs) and never mislabelled as past board exam papers.
  • Where an official date sheet has not yet been announced by the Controller of Examinations, sample or prototype timetable rows are unambiguously labelled as DEMO — NOT AN OFFICIAL ANNOUNCEMENT (when enabled for UI testing) or marked Awaiting Official Release.
