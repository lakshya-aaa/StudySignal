3. Handling of Demo, Model Paper & Missing Data

In accordance with our strict data transparency policies:

  • We do not invent or fabricate exam dates, notifications, PYQ links, cutoffs, or circular numbers.
  • Official Model Papers (such as BSEB 2026 Model Question Papers or UPMSP Model Papers) and CBT mock/practice links (such as NTA Practice Portals or CUET UG answer keys) are explicitly separated from downloadable Previous Year Question Papers (PYQs).
  • Where an official date sheet or exam cycle has not yet been announced (such as 2027 schedules for JEE Advanced, NEET UG, CUET UG, BSEB, or CBSE), StudySignal unambiguously displays verified reference cycles alongside Awaiting Official Release status.
