How to authenticate official CBSE circulars

Official CBSE circulars always possess:

  • A distinct circular reference number (e.g. CBSE/ACAD/... or COORD/EXAM/...).
  • Authorized digital signature of the Controller of Examinations or Director (Academics).
  • Direct presence on cbseacademic.nic.in/circulars.html or cbse.gov.in.
