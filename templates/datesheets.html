Official CBSE Examination Day Instructions

  1. Entry Cut-off: No candidate shall be permitted to enter the examination centre after 10:00 AM IST under any circumstances.
  2. Admit Card & ID: Candidates must carry their school ID card and printed Admit Card with parent and school principal signatures.
  3. Question Paper Reading: Question papers will be handed out at 10:15 AM. 15 minutes are strictly reserved for reading the questions.
  4. Writing Commences: Candidates can start writing answers only at 10:30 AM IST.
  5. Prohibited Items: Electronic gadgets, mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, and rough notes are strictly forbidden inside the hall.
