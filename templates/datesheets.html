CBSE Standard Examination Day Guidelines (Reference Protocols)

The guidelines below reflect CBSE's standard examination protocols observed across previous board sessions. Candidates must verify their individual session admit card and the final session date sheet circular issued on cbse.gov.in for definitive, subject-specific timings.

  1. Centre Entry Protocol: In standard morning sessions, examination centre gates typically close at 10:00 AM IST. Candidates are advised to report to their designated centre at least 30–45 minutes prior to gate closing.
  2. Mandatory Documents: Candidates must present an authentic, printed CBSE Admit Card (duly signed by the candidate, parent/guardian, and school principal) along with their official school identity card.
  3. Question Paper Reading Window: Under standard morning session schedules, question papers are distributed at 10:15 AM IST to provide a strict 15-minute reading period (10:15 AM to 10:30 AM) prior to writing answers.
  4. Writing Schedule: Writing commences at the designated session start time (standard 10:30 AM IST for morning papers). Final session timings are confirmed directly on the official admit card.
  5. Prohibited Materials: Electronic gadgets, mobile phones, smartwatches, programmable calculators, bags, and unauthorized written materials are strictly prohibited inside the examination centre.
