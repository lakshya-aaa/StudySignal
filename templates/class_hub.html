CBSE Examination Guidelines for {{ class_title }}

Essential official protocols every board examinee must follow:

1. Reporting Time

Candidates must report to their allotted examination centre before 10:00 AM IST. Entry gates strictly close as per board directions.

2. Reading Time

Question papers are distributed at 10:15 AM. Students receive 15 minutes of uninterrupted reading time before writing begins at 10:30 AM.

3. Official Admit Card

Always carry the original CBSE Admit Card signed by the School Principal. Private candidates must download their verified admit card from cbse.gov.in.
