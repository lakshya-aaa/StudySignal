Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At ExamPulse India ("we", "our", or "the portal"), accessible from our website, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by ExamPulse India and how we utilize it.

1. Information We Collect

ExamPulse India does not require mandatory student user registration, logins, or payment details. Visitors can browse all CBSE syllabus pages, date sheets, and circulars completely anonymously.

  • Voluntary Form Submissions: If you contact us via our Contact Page, we may collect your name, email address, subject, and the contents of your message solely to respond to your inquiry.
  • Standard Log Files: Like most web servers, our hosting infrastructure logs visitors when they visit websites. These logs include IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, and referring/exit pages. This information is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

ExamPulse India uses standard browser cookies to store visitor preferences and optimize web performance. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.

3. Third-Party Advertising Partners

Some third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense or compliant educational sponsors) may serve ads on our portal. These third-party ad servers or networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that appear on ExamPulse India, which are sent directly to your browser.

  • Third-party ad vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

4. External Links to Official CBSE Websites

ExamPulse India contains direct links to third-party government websites, primarily cbse.gov.in and cbseacademic.nic.in. Please note that we do not have control over the privacy policies and practices of these external sites. We strongly encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

5. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. ExamPulse India provides purely academic curricula, official circulars, and study blueprints and does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

6. Consent & Updates

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. Any revisions will be posted transparently on this page.
