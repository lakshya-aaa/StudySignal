Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At StudySignal ("we", "our", or "the portal"), accessible from our website, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines our data practices, what information is processed, and our commitment to user privacy.

1. Information We Collect

StudySignal is an open, free-access educational guide. We do not require mandatory student registration, user logins, profiles, or payment details. Visitors can access and browse all board syllabus pages (CBSE, UP Board, CISCE, BSEB, MSBSHSE) and competitive examination portals (JEE Main, JEE Advanced, NEET UG, CUET UG) completely anonymously without creating an account.

  • Voluntary Form Submissions: If you contact us via our Contact Page, you may provide your name, email address, subject, and the contents of your message. This voluntary information is used solely to evaluate user feedback or report issues such as broken official document links.
  • Standard Log Files: Like most web servers, our hosting infrastructure automatically records standard technical access logs when visitors request web pages. These logs may include IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, and referring/exit pages. This standard server telemetry is used for server health, DDoS prevention, and security monitoring, and is not linked to personal student identities.

2. Cookies, Tracking & Analytics Disclosures

StudySignal currently operates as a cookie-free website.

  • No Tracking or Session Cookies: The application does not set first-party tracking cookies, login session cookies, or persistent profiling cookies on your browser.
  • No Analytics Scripts: Our portal currently does not run client-side analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics) or third-party behavioral trackers.
  • Future Feature Updates: If cookie-based features (such as saved user preferences, bookmarking, or performance analytics) are introduced in future releases, this policy will be updated with explicit disclosures prior to their launch.

3. Third-Party Advertising Status & Future Disclosures

Third-party advertising is currently completely disabled across StudySignal.

  • Current Status: Our web pages do not currently load external advertising scripts (such as Google AdSense or programmatic ad exchanges), and no third-party advertising cookies or tracking beacons are set on your device.
  • Future Advertising Policy: To maintain free and open educational access without paywalls, StudySignal may apply to participate in third-party advertising programs (such as Google AdSense) in the future. If third-party advertising is introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated prior to activation to describe:
    • How third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites across the Internet.
    • How Google's advertising cookies enable it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their browsing visits.
    • Available user choices to opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings ↗ or the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out portal at aboutads.info/choices ↗.
  • Advertising Privacy Resources: Users interested in learning how Google manages data in its advertising products can review Google's Advertising Privacy & Technologies documentation ↗.
  • No Guaranteed Approval: Any future ad integration remains subject to independent policy review and technical approval by third-party ad networks. No commercial advertising is active on this portal today.

4. External Links to Official Board & Examination Websites

StudySignal provides direct hyperlinks to official statutory education boards and national examination portals, including cbse.gov.in, cbseacademic.nic.in, upmsp.edu.in, cisce.org, biharboardonline.com, mahahsscboard.in, maa.ac.in, nta.ac.in, jeemain.nta.nic.in, jeeadv.ac.in, josaa.nic.in, neet.nta.nic.in, nmc.org.in, mcc.nic.in, aaccc.gov.in, and cuet.nta.nic.in. Please note that we do not have control over the privacy policies, security practices, or content of external government and authority portals. We encourage users to review the privacy policy of any third-party website they visit.

5. Children's and Minor Students' Privacy

Protecting the digital privacy of students and young learners is a primary priority for StudySignal. Our portal provides publicly available academic curricula, study outlines, and official circular references.

StudySignal does not require account creation, student profiling, or commercial transactions from visitors of any age. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from minors. Because our portal serves Secondary (Class 10) and Senior Secondary (Class 12) students—many of whom are under eighteen years of age—we encourage parents, guardians, and educators to guide and monitor student online activities.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a student under your care has submitted personal data through our feedback form without guidance, please notify us through our Contact Page so we can take steps to remove any submitted information.

Statutory Notice: Data privacy standards concerning minors in India are governed by applicable national legislation, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Definitive regulatory rules and compliance guidance remain subject to forthcoming government notification and independent legal counsel.

6. Privacy Inquiries & Policy Updates

StudySignal is committed to ongoing transparency. Any revisions to this Privacy Policy will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date. For privacy-related inquiries or feedback, please reach out via our Contact Page.
