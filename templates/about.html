About StudySignal

Bridging the information gap between official statutory boards and millions of Indian students.

1. Our Mission

Every year, tens of millions of students across India appear for Secondary (Class 10) and Senior Secondary / Intermediate / HSC (Class 12) board examinations conducted by national and state authorities. Despite boards publishing official curricula, model papers, timetables, and circulars online, students and parents frequently encounter unverified rumours, broken links, fake timetables, and paywalled education portals.

StudySignal is an independent, free-access educational information portal created to provide a clean, fast, and accessible digital reference directory that links directly to verified official board documents without clutter or unverified claims.

2. Supported Education Boards & What We Verify

StudySignal currently indexes and structures verified public educational documents across five active Indian school boards for Class 10 and Class 12 learners:

  • CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education): 2026–27 subject syllabi from cbseacademic.nic.in, circulars from cbse.gov.in, timetable advisories, and 2014–2025 PYQs.
  • UP Board (UPMSP): High School & Intermediate 2026–27 subject syllabi, 23 verified sample papers, Class 9 Question Banks, confirmed February 2027 exam timetable, and circulars on upmsp.edu.in.
  • CISCE (ICSE Class 10 & ISC Class 12): Verified regulations, subject syllabi, timetables, circulars, and 2017–2027 PYQs from cisce.org.
  • Bihar Board (BSEB): Consolidated Matric & +2 syllabi, passing regulations, 57 verified 2026 Model Question Papers (explicitly distinguished from past exam PYQs), 2027 timetable status + 2026 official reference programmes, and notices from biharboardonline.com.
  • Maharashtra Board (MSBSHSE & SCERT Maharashtra): Confirmed Feb–Mar 2027 SSC & HSC exam timetables, 132 PYQ entries / 332 medium PDFs from mahahsscboard.in, SCERT Maharashtra Prashnapedhi question banks on maa.ac.in, Balbharati e-books on books.ebalbharati.in, and 246 official subject codes.

3. Independent, Free-Access Approach

We believe foundational education information must remain completely free and lightweight. StudySignal is built using zero-dependency, ultra-fast web standards that load seamlessly on both affordable mobile devices and rural broadband connections across India.

4. Editorial Integrity & Corrections

We treat educational accuracy with the highest degree of responsibility:

  • We never invent, fabricate, or predict examination dates, syllabus alterations, or missing PYQ archives. Where an official board does not publish past PYQs or question banks (for example, UPMSP High School PYQs or BSEB Question Banks), StudySignal states that absence transparently.
  • Whenever mock or sample data is used during development or before official announcements, it is explicitly labelled as demo data.
  • If you notice any broken link or discrepancy with an official board notification, please report it immediately through our Contact Page.
